# Mixamo download checklist

Download these **seven** FBX files only. Use a neutral human character, one clean cyclic repetition, and an **FBX Binary, without skin** download when the MOVA Blender rig will supply the visible body. Rename each downloaded file exactly as shown and put it in `motion/source/`.

- [ ] `mixamo_squat_loop.fbx`
  - Search: `bodyweight squat`, `squat`
  - Choose: a centred bilateral standing squat with fixed feet, one smooth down-and-up cycle.
  - Avoid: jump squats, dance squats, barbell/dumbbell props, turns, walk-ins, camera motion.
  - Reused by: `BAR_SQUAT`, `BAR_SQUAT_CURL`, `BW_SQUAT`, `HB_SQUAT`, `HB_SQUAT_CURL`, `MINI_SQUAT`, `MINI_SQUAT_SIDE_STEP`.

- [ ] `mixamo_hip_hinge_loop.fbx`
  - Search: `hip hinge`, `bend over`, `bend`
  - Choose: a controlled standing hip-back bend with soft knees and a neutral spine.
  - Avoid: floor pick-ups, deep lunges, twists, fast bowing, held props.
  - Reused by: `BAR_DEADLIFT`, `BAR_HINGE`, `BW_HINGE`, `HB_HINGE`, `MINI_HINGE`.

- [ ] `mixamo_standing_row_loop.fbx`
  - Search: `standing row`, `row`, `pull`
  - Choose: a symmetric standing pull toward the lower ribs; a slight hinge is acceptable.
  - Avoid: seated rowing, one-arm pulls, oar/boat rowing, a visible barbell, stepping or rotation.
  - Reused by: `BAR_ROW`, `BAR_HINGE_ROW`, `HB_ROW`, `HB_HINGE_ROW`.

- [ ] `mixamo_bicep_curl_loop.fbx`
  - Search: `bicep curl`, `curl`
  - Choose: a bilateral standing curl with elbows held near the torso.
  - Avoid: walking curls, alternating arms, kettlebells/dumbbells, momentum swings.
  - Reused by: `BAR_CURL`, `HB_CURL`.

- [ ] `mixamo_front_raise_loop.fbx`
  - Search: `front raise`, `standing arm raise`, `raise arms`
  - Choose: a bilateral, controlled straight-arm raise from the thighs to chest/shoulder level.
  - Avoid: overhead press, lateral raise, waving, large torso lean, props.
  - Reused by: `BAR_FRONT_RAISE`, `HB_FRONT_RAISE`, `BW_ARM_SWEEP`.

- [ ] `mixamo_march_in_place_loop.fbx`
  - Search: `march in place`, `marching`
  - Choose: a centred alternating knee-lift loop with no forward travel.
  - Avoid: jogging, high knees, combat marching, turning, forward walk cycles.
  - Reused by: `BW_MARCH`, `MINI_MARCH`, `MINI_WIDE_MARCH`.

- [ ] `mixamo_lateral_step_loop.fbx`
  - Search: `side step`, `lateral step`, `side shuffle`
  - Choose: controlled upright lateral movement that can be root-centred into a loop.
  - Avoid: dance moves, spins, kicks, defensive shuffles, long travel.
  - Reused by: `BW_LATERAL_STEP`, `MINI_LATERAL_STEP`, `MINI_LATERAL_TAP`.
